The problem

CRM in healthcare is not batch sending. It is recognizing the same patient across email, WhatsApp and push, all orchestrated from HubSpot, and answering the question the business actually asks: how much revenue did this campaign bring in.

I work at the intersection of two worlds that rarely meet: the engagement event volume coming out of HubSpot and the channels it fires, and the transactional base of bookings, visits and paid treatment plans. That intersection is what turns an open rate into proven revenue, and what makes personalization at scale possible without losing track of who received what.

The model joins the engagement data HubSpot orchestrates across the three channels with the network's real transactional data. The key is the normalized CPF, which links whoever received the message to whoever actually booked, showed up and paid. First touch deduplication per template makes sure the same patient is never counted twice when reached by more than one channel, which keeps the number honest.

What a CPF is. Brazil's individual taxpayer registry number: a single, government-issued identifier that belongs to one person for life, roughly the equivalent of a U.S. Social Security number. Because a patient carries the same CPF across every clinic, channel and system in the network, it is the one key that can join engagement data to the transactional base — which is what makes the deduplication above trustworthy.
02

Process automation

MCP workflow for email template builds

Before: finished design in Figma, HTML assembled by hand, manual setup in HubSpot.

Every new email cost someone on the team half a day. The bottleneck was not creative, it was transport: pulling the layout out of Figma, rebuilding the structure in HTML, uploading assets, recreating all of it inside the HubSpot editor.

I built an MCP workflow that connects both ends. Design and asset extraction come straight from Figma, the HTML is assembled automatically and the template lands in HubSpot without going through the editor. The team moved to where it should have been all along, reviewing.

Before, per template 6 hours
After, via MCP 30 minutes

From prompt to registered template, human review included. Over 90% reduction in execution time, with both bars drawn on the same scale.

03

Patient experience

Redirect page to the right clinic's WhatsApp

The problem: a patient clicked the campaign and then had to figure out their own clinic's WhatsApp number.

A large network means a lot of contact numbers. Any friction at this point kills conversion, because the patient has already shown intent and still has to go looking. Together with the design manager I built a redirect page that resolves the clinic automatically and drops the patient into the right WhatsApp conversation.

Resolution works by service unit, so the same page serves both medical and dental without duplicating structure. It runs integrated with a HubSpot landing page, so the campaign team works with a single link.

Less friction at the exact moment the patient has already decided to book.
